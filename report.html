How this works: every night at about 3 am the SafeStop server counts public toilets on OpenStreetMap near 20 busy areas, plus the unsafe spots and closed places reported by SafeStop users. Map data is only as complete as OpenStreetMap; reports come from anonymous users and are not verified. Use this to spot gaps and patterns – for example to request toilets or streetlights from BMC – not as proof of a specific incident.
